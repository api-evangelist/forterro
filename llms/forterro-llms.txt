# Forterro

> Forterro is a pan-European industrial software company (founded 2012, headquartered in London) that provides enterprise resource planning (ERP) and specialised business software to the industrial mid-market. It operates a portfolio of regional and vertical ERP brands and point solutions serving more than 13,000 industrial businesses across manufacturing, warehousing, wholesale, and retail. Forterro does not publish a unified public developer/API portal or OpenAPI definitions; APIs are delivered per product (for example the cloud-native Fortee platform's "open APIs" and product-specific supply-chain integration APIs) and are typically accessed through the relevant product or by contacting Forterro.
